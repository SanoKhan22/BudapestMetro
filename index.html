Budapest Metro by -Ehsanullah

Game Rules

How to Play:

Build 4 metro lines (M1, M2, M3, M4) across Budapest's 10×10 grid.

Controls:

  • Draw Card: Click "Draw Card" button
  • Build Segment: Click starting station, then click target station
  • Skip Turn: Click "Skip" to draw next card without building

Rules:

  • Segments must be at 90° or 45° angles
  • Cannot intersect other segments (except at stations)
  • Cannot pass through intermediate stations
  • Cannot revisit stations on same line (no loops)
  • Only one segment between any two stations

Scoring:

  • Round: (Districts × Max in District) + Danube Crossings
  • Railway: Points for visiting train stations
  • Junctions: 2pts (2 lines), 5pts (3 lines), 9pts (4 lines)
